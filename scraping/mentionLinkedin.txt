Attention all Google Docs users! How many precious minutes of your life have you wasted adjusting the margins just to make that pesky page break disappear? Well, say goodbye to that frustration because the new pageless mode in Google Docs has arrived! That's right, you can now create documents with no page breaks, giving you more time to waste on other trivial settings. But let's be real, we all know we're going to spend that time perfecting the background color (please don't) and setting it as our default. Don't worry, I won't judge.

For those of you who are just here to learn how to remove a page break, the process is simple. Just double-click on the page break and voila! Your pages will seamlessly come together without any awkward gaps or dotted lines. And trust me, your documents will look much more polished without those pesky lines.

But wait, there's more! With the pageless format, you also have the option to customize the page width. Go wild and choose between Narrow, Medium, Wide, or Full.
