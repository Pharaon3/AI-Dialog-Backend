Hey LinkedIn fam, let's talk about something we can all relate to - the struggle of adjusting margins in Google Docs just to make page breaks look less awkward. 🤦‍♀️ But guess what? We can now waste our time adjusting other trivial app settings instead because Google Docs has a new pageless mode! 🎉 That's right, no more page breaks to deal with. Here's how to make the switch: Go to File > Page setup, select Pageless, and press OK. And while you're at it, have some fun and change the background color (or don't, your choice). 💁‍♀️ Want all your Google Docs to be pageless? Just click Set as default. Oh, and if you're wondering how to remove page breaks in Google Docs, just double-click on them. But wait, there's more! The lack of page breaks also means no more annoying horizontal dotted line. 🙌 And let's be real, that makes our documents look a little more polished. Plus, images will automatically adjust to the size of your screen. 📷 So no more giant or tiny images, it's just right. But don't worry, if you're using pageless mode for a standard document
